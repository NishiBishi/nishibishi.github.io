About

I'm Steven, a technical artist who learns games by building inside them. My current playground is Stellar Blade, where I've been crafting an entire ARPG endgame as a mod, and a custom UE 5.8 world grown out of that work. I care about systems that prove themselves with numbers and environments that tell stories, and I build my own tools (procedural generators, agentic pipelines) to get there faster.
